## 1. 阶段目标

```text

页面不靠开发临时决定按钮
```

# 15. 权限命名约定

```text
{scene}:{module}:{action}
```

示例：

```text
guarantee:application:view
guarantee:application:create
guarantee:application:review
guarantee:contract:edit
guarantee:contract:effective:no_approval
guarantee:billing:adjust
guarantee:billing:pay
guarantee:device:bind
guarantee:device:import
```

数据范围与权限分离：

```text
权限决定能不能做
数据范围决定能做哪些数据
```
